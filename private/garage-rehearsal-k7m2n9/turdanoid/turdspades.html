TurdSpades

Classic 4-player Spades: you + North partner vs West/East bots.

  • Bid 1-13 tricks, or call Nil and try to take zero.
  • You must follow lead suit when possible.
  • Spades are trump and cannot lead until broken, unless your hand is all spades.
  • Make bid: +10 per bid trick, plus +1 per overtrick bag.
  • Nil succeeds for +100 or fails for -100. Your tricks still help the team contract and can become bags.
  • Every 10 bags = -100 penalty.

Controls: choose a bid with +/- or press N for Nil, then Lock Bid. During play, select a card then Play Selected. Press Enter to confirm bid/play.
