Chess, with a little help.

Royal Flush Chess uses mature open-source chess software. No remote engine service, accounts or paid API is involved.

  • chess.js 1.4.0 by Jeff Hlywa and contributors provides legal moves and notation. BSD-2-Clause license and copyright notice.

Practice, at your pace

Difficulty names describe bounded engine searches, not tested Elo ratings. There is no clock or competitive leaderboard. Hints and takebacks are counted in the result. Standard material draws are detected; exhaustive recognition of every blocked dead position is not claimed.

Rules reference: FIDE Laws of Chess, articles 5 and 9. Repetition and 50-move claims are optional; fivefold repetition and 75 moves are automatic, with checkmate taking precedence.
